## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* BRIGHTEN
  * Research project
    * Uses 3 different apps which were accessible by a phone
      * iPST - App based on problem solving technology
      * Project EVO - Video game to train skills
      * Health Tips - Provided health tips daily
    * Study lasted for 12 weeks
    * Compensated for participating 
  * [Link](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/news/science-updates/2016/a-bright-technological-future-for-mental-health-trials)

* AI study (by NAMI)
  * Research project
    * Current Problems
      * Can be helpful, confusing or dangerous
      * Not always accurate
      * Not a replacement for care
      * 12% of adults are likely to use AI instead of consulting a therapist (1% already do)
    * Goal
      * Make AI safer to use
      * Provide easy to use AI chats
  * [Link](https://www.nami.org/ai-and-mental-health/)
##### Hardware
* TouchPoint
* 2 devices with clothing clips
  * 3 built in speeds to help with stress relief
    * Slow speed for sleep/meditation
    * Medium speed to reduce stress and regain focus
    * Fast speed to reduce emotional agitation and manage anger
  * $219.99 USD (quite expensive)
  * [Link](https://www.thetouchpointsolution.com/products/touchpoints-for-calm)
